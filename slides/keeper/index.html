KEEPER / FAMILY MEMORY STORYBOOKS

A little of their world,
kept in yours.

Record a loved one’s memories. Or tell the stories you remember of them. Turn those words into a book your family can keep.

Built by Alex Picon · alexnpc@me.com

01 / THE REASON

They lived in a world
we never got to see.

Family history lives in ordinary details: a kitchen, a journey, a phrase repeated for years. Those details deserve more than a forgotten audio file.

Keeper starts with one memory. If the person is no longer here, it preserves the family member’s recollection and names who remembers it.

Original words stay available. Narration uses a clearly labeled reading voice, not a cloned loved one.

LIMA, PERÚ / THE STORIES THAT FORMED US

Work. Bread.
Faith. Family.

A father works at the docks and brings home bread. His four-year-old daughter thanks him, then asks for butter. He leaves in tears. A grandmother walks home praying to la Virgen del Carmen de la Legua in Callao and finds the bill that brings help.

Grandma narrates: her husband, their little girl, her prayer, and her faith. The family preserves her telling; the grandchild's different interpretation stays in a separate note.

Companion stories about port workers and family care are explicitly labeled as inspired fiction. These are written recollections; generated narration is never presented as a real family recording.

Grandma’s telling: 8 short chapters, 8 distinct illustrations · English and Spanish text · all 16 Grandma chapter readings prebuilt. Illustrations are interpretations, not portraits.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

One small story.
A place to begin.

  1. Record, upload, or write. ElevenLabs Scribe transcribes family audio. Click a word to hear that moment again.
  2. Tell it all. Thirty minutes of uninterrupted recording. Optional storytelling help stays tucked away.
  3. Keep their telling. Choose eight or twelve chapters. Short chapters are the default, with the original storyteller in first person.

Actual capture form: original storyteller, person preserving the telling, recording/upload, chapter length, and tone controls.

03 / THE LIVE DEMO

A keepsake with

Grandma tells her own story in eight short chapters, each with a distinct illustration. Her husband, their daughter, her prayer and faith stay in her perspective. The family member preserving it is identified separately.

04 / WHAT IS REAL

The source stays.
The family has the last word.

  • The whole original story stays separate from the imaginative book. Exact source anchors are validated.
  • Each chapter identifies invented scenes and dialogue. Incomplete books preserve the original telling.
  • Editing a page resets its review status.
  • IndexedDB keeps drafts, books, and audio on this browser.
  • Export contains all chapters, source text, images, and any recording.

Validated with API regression tests and browser acceptance of live voice services, read-along timing, review gates and offline audio export. This tests implementation, not historical accuracy or user outcomes. AI prose still requires family review.

05 / ACROSS GENERATIONS AND LANGUAGES

Same memory.
Another way in.

Grandma Rachel in English. Abuela Tina in Spanish. Five target languages. The original always stays alongside.

Grok translates the edition; ElevenLabs narrates it with word-level timing. Each translated edition has its own review step. Changing the source invalidates old translations and generated audio.
